How verification works

Three steps, no account required for the person checking a credential.

01

Enter the certificate ID

Type or paste the ID printed on the certificate into the verification field.

02

We check the issuing partner's registry

Certyvault routes the ID to the partner institution that issued it and checks it against their record of active credentials.

03

Get a clear result

See the certificate holder, course, and issue date, or a clear explanation if nothing matches.

Why institutions partner with Certyvault

A verification page is often the only interaction a hiring manager has with a training provider's brand. As an independent verification partner, we hold every result to that standard: accurate, fast, and easy to trust on first glance.

Every result is pulled live from the issuing partner's own registry rather than a cached export, so revoked or expired certificates never show as valid.
